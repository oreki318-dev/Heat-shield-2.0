Clinical Heat Illness Triage

Emergency Protocol
Stage 1 • Mild

Heat Cramps

Profuse sweating, involuntary muscle fasciculations in calves/abdomen.

First Aid: Rest in shaded cooling area; administer oral rehydration salts (ORS).
Stage 2 • Moderate

Heat Exhaustion

Orthostatic hypotension, core temp 38–40°C, tachycardia, pale clammy skin.

First Aid: Transfer to AC room; apply conductive evaporative cooling towels.
Stage 3 • CRITICAL MEDICAL EMERGENCY

Heat Stroke

Core temp > 40°C, delirium/coma, hot dry erythematous skin.

URGENT: CALL 112 / 911 IMMEDIATELY. Whole-body ice water immersion.
